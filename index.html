⚙ What I Do

I'm Farhan — a self-taught modder, cracker, developer, and gamer. I live at the intersection of curiosity and code, where the urge to tear software apart meets the discipline to build something new from the pieces.

As a modder, I take games apart and reshape them. As a cracker, I reverse-engineer binaries to understand protection schemes. As a developer, I build tools and apps that solve real problems.
